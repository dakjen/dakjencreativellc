What It Is

You compile the content. We make it look like it came from a firm with a marketing department.

Full graphic design and formatting for RFP responses — cover pages, section dividers, color scheme, layout, the whole visual package — plus a light editing pass. You're writing the narrative, pulling résumés, and coordinating subs while running the work you already have. Send us the compiled draft; we send back a proposal that reads and looks the part.

What you send us

  • Your compiled draft — in whatever state it's in
  • The solicitation — so we format to its structure and limits
  • Company materials and team résumés — so the proposal is complete, not just pretty
  • Your logo, if you have one, and the deadline

What you get back

  • A designed proposal — cover, dividers, and a color and type system on every page
  • Formatted content — headings, tables, callouts, and résumés that read at a glance
  • A light edit — consistency, terminology, and the Sunday-night typos
  • Portal-ready PDF, plus editable source
  • Optional add-ons you keep — designed résumés, project profiles, a capability statement — reusable in every proposal after this one

How it works

  1. Send the brief below — your firm, the solicitation, the deadline, and your member code.
  2. We confirm scope and member pricing in writing, usually within one business day.
  3. Send the compiled draft. We design, format, and edit.
  4. You review, we revise, you submit.
