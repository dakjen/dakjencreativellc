Why It's Built This Way

Senior people on your account — not a bench you never meet.

Most studios sell you the founder and staff you with juniors. We don't run that way. Dakotah Jennifer sets the strategy, writes the positioning, and stays on the account — supported by a team that handles execution across digital, communications, and coordination so nothing sits waiting.

That structure comes from working inside a real business, not just alongside one. Dakotah is Chief Creative Officer at Northern Real Estate Urban Ventures and Senior Managing Director of Project Community Capital®, so she reads a deal, a budget, and a lease-up schedule the way her clients do.

The result: work that's sharp enough to stand up in a pitch and practical enough to actually ship.
