$1,000–$1,500 one-time

A review of your workflows, your tools, and your team. You leave with a short, prioritized roadmap: where AI earns its place, where it does not, and the risks worth naming up front — data, privacy, and how your staff actually feel about it.

Advisory
$1,500–$2,500/mo

A steady hand on the strategy.

Tier 2
Advisory + Setup

Built, not just recommended.

Tier 3
Embedded AI Leadership
$5,500–$8,000/mo

Your AI lead in the room — steering, not staffing.

  • Everything in Tiers 1 & 2
  • A standing seat where AI decisions get made
  • Adoption strategy across teams — who uses what, and how you know it is working
  • Automation architecture — we design it and oversee the build
  • Governance, risk, and vendor calls as they come up
  • Weekly access

Tool and subscription costs are yours, or passed through at cost. Hands-on build and done-for-you production are available alongside any tier, scoped and capped separately. Every engagement includes a written data-handling clause covering which of your data goes into which tools.
